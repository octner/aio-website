## What AIO is

The Australian Internet Observatory is a national research infrastructure facility established in 2024 through the Australian Government’s National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS) and supported through co-investment from our partners.

Our mission is to build an interconnected ecosystem of people, data, and tools that enables research on digital platforms, applications and emerging technologies in the public interest.

AIO works with Australian and international partners to deliver on four core pillars: a research platform, data collection tools and methods, data governance and outreach.

## Why AIO exists

The next decade will see a new wave of digital transformation and many challenges in the way data, communication and digital platforms operate. Large-scale online data has the potential to substantially contribute to the capacity of business, government and the community to understand and address Australia’s major social, economic and public policy challenges.

The Australian Internet Observatory is developing the tools and capabilities required to gather and analyse online user experience data, algorithms, and interactions. It supports innovative approaches to the collection and analysis of digital social data and internet platforms and the analytical tools and governance required to support cutting-edge research on social, economic, health and environmental issues.

## Four core pillars

- **Digital Research Platform.** A full research workflow platform. Manage your project end-to-end, from participant recruitment through data collection and analysis. [About the AIO Platform](/platform/)
- **Data Collection Tools.** An integrated suite of data collection tools and methods, including data donation, download packages, mobile streaming capture, browser extensions, APIs and large language models. [Compare the data collection tools](/platform/data-collection-tools/)
- **Data governance and management.** Ethical and legal frameworks and guides for working with digital research methods and social data, including guidance for Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) applications. [Read the research ethics guide](/learn/resources/ethics-guide/)
- **Outreach and training.** Supporting research training and engagement through workshops, events, training and online resources. [See the training](/learn/training/)

## Partners and funding

The Australian Internet Observatory (AIO) is a co-investment partnership with the Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC) through the HASS and Indigenous Research Data Commons ([DOI: 10.3565/hjrp-b141](https://doi.org/10.3565/hjrp-b141)). The ARDC is enabled by the Australian Government's National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS).

### University partners

AIO is delivered by six Australian university partners:

- RMIT University
- Queensland University of Technology
- University of Queensland
- University of Melbourne
- Swinburne University
- Deakin University

### Collaboration

AIO is developed in collaboration with researchers, research centres, and organisations across Australia and internationally including the ARC Centre of Excellence for Automated Decision Making + Society.
