AIO collaborates with partner institutions to give researchers access to additional APIs and web archives. One of these is the AIReD dashboard, hosted at the University of Melbourne. It provides large-scale access to social media data and archived platform content.

The AIO training material (Module 7) describes AIReD as a web-based dashboard for researchers at Australian academic institutions, who sign in with their institutional credentials. It offers aggregation, topic modelling, term analysis, text search and a chatbot, without writing code. Because of platform restrictions, it gives the IDs of matching posts rather than their text.
