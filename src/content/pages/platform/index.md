The AIO Platform is a national self-service research infrastructure developed by the Australian Internet Observatory (AIO). Researchers can focus on designing rigorous and ethical studies, rather than building the underlying technology from scratch.

![The home and login page of the AIO Platform](https://internetobservatory.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AIO-Platform-Homepage-Login-page-scaled.png)

## Who it is for

The AIO Platform is designed for humanities and social sciences researchers, including those without advanced programming skills.

It initially supports data donation research using Data Download Packages (DDPs). DDPs are personal data archives which can be requested from social media, streaming and other digital services. They may include viewing and listening histories, searches, interactions, advertising information, account settings and other records of an individual's experience.

[Read the Data Download Packages explainer](/learn/resources/data-download-packages-explainer/)

## What researchers can do

Researchers at Australian universities sign in to the AIO Platform with their institutional credentials. Researchers can:

- create and register a research project
- configure data donation activities
- use supported Data Download Package tools
- manage research participants and donated data
- collaborate with approved members of their research team
- access AIO guidance, resources and training.

## Ethics approval

Projects involving human participants or donated personal data require institutional ethics approval before data collection begins, in line with AIO's ethics-first approach.

The AIO Platform uses access controls and secure Australian-hosted infrastructure to manage donated research data. Approved projects must follow applicable research ethics, privacy, consent and data-management requirements.

[Read the research ethics guide](/learn/resources/ethics-guide/)

## Before you apply for funding or ethics approval

Researchers considering a project are encouraged to contact the AIO before submitting a funding application or seeking ethics approval. The team can then provide advice about suitable tools, project requirements and research design.
