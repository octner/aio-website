Learning how to work with digital media data can be daunting. The scale and diversity of data, along with the challenge of accessing and collecting data, require some time to master. However, it is still possible to gain an understanding and foundational knowledge about methods for working with digital platform data, and to integrate them into your research over a short period.

AIO training comes in three forms: online modules (listed at the end of this page), workshops and events, and individual consultations. For guides, explainers and videos, see the [learning resources](/learn/resources/).

## Workshops, presentations and webinars

The AIO team regularly delivers hands-on training, workshops, and presentations at major national events, summer schools, and conferences. Recent examples include:

- ResBaz Queensland: interactive workshops on data donation tools, web archiving, and the AIO dashboard.
- Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC) HASS and Indigenous Research Data Commons events: presentations and training at the annual Summer School.
- Webinars and special sessions, such as [Doing Digital Platforms Research with the Australian Internet Observatory](/news/webinar-doing-digital-platform-research-with-aio/), covering data donation, mixed-methods approaches, interviews, and surveys.
- Conferences (AANZCA, APSA, SciDataCon, and others).

The sessions are collaborative, practical, and suitable for researchers at different levels.

[See upcoming and past events](/events/)

## Summer Institute for Computational Social Science

In 2026, AIO and the ARC Centre of Excellence for Automated Decision-Making and Society (ADM+S) co-hosted the Summer Institute for Computational Social Science (SICSS) in Melbourne. The school ran from 22 June to 3 July 2026. It brought together PhD candidates and early-career researchers for intensive training in computational methods for studying society.
