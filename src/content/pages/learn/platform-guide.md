Whether you are a qualitative researcher exploring digital data for the first time or a computational social scientist looking for more data, we are here to support you.

1. **Define your research question.** Before exploring tools, clarify what you want to study and what type of evidence you need. Use the learning resources and relevant research to identify which modules and tools best meet your needs. If you are not sure what the best method or course of action is, reach out to us.
   [Browse the learning resources](/learn/resources/) · [Contact the AIO team](/about/contact/)
2. **Take a closer look at the AIO tools.** Match your research design to our infrastructure and explore the suite of tools we are offering. Consider which tools and methods match your research question, what type of data you need, how they contribute to other methods, and whether they align with your study design.
   [Compare the data collection tools](/platform/data-collection-tools/)
3. **Contact the AIO before you apply.** Contact the AIO before you submit a funding application or seek ethics approval. The team can then provide advice about suitable tools, project requirements and research design.
   [Contact the AIO team](/about/contact/)
4. **Get ethics approval.** If your research involves human participants or their data, you will need approval from a Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) before data collection begins. The research ethics guide has sample paragraphs that show how key sections of an ethics application for digital media research might be drafted.
   [Read the research ethics guide](/learn/resources/ethics-guide/)
5. **Engage with our training.** The AIO training program offers multiple formats, including training materials in the learning resources, in-person workshops, online events, and video modules. The AIO provides one-on-one engagement to help researchers answer questions and design studies using different tools.
   [See the training](/learn/training/)
6. **Create a project.** Researchers at Australian universities sign in to the AIO Platform with their institutional credentials. Use the AIO Platform to create a new project, enrol participants, and manage donated data.
   [Launch the AIO Platform](https://app.internetobservatory.org.au/) · [Read what the AIO Platform does](/platform/)
