## Our goal

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. WCAG is the accessibility standard of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

## Report a problem

If you cannot use part of this website, or you find an accessibility problem, email [contact@internetobservatory.org.au](mailto:contact@internetobservatory.org.au). Tell us the address of the page and what went wrong.
