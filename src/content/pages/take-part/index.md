Your data can make a difference. Take part in meaningful research through the Australian Internet Observatory’s secure platform.

## Why your data matters

Digital platforms shape how we communicate, find information, and view the world. This includes a variety of businesses that range from:

- social media like Facebook and TikTok
- digital streaming like Netflix and Spotify
- AI-powered chat interfaces like ChatGPT
- services like Uber, Doordash, and AirBnB.

Digital platforms use hidden algorithms to recommend videos and songs, provide information, and determine prices for goods and services.

By sharing your data and participating in our research, you can help Australian researchers study these platforms and the way our everyday interactions with them impact on our lives. Your contribution helps us better understand how algorithms work, which kinds of advertisements target Australians, and how social media affects our society.

## What data donation is

Digital data donation helps researchers understand how people actually live, communicate, and make meaning in a digital world. By choosing to share data such as data download packages or information collected through apps or browser tools, individuals enable studies of everyday online activities like searching, streaming, and using social media that are challenging to collect with traditional research methods.

The benefits of data donation extend to both researchers and participants. By participating in data donation research, you can also learn more about your digital footprint.

## What taking part involves

Data donation involves a few simple, voluntary steps. If you choose to participate in a data donation study, you will first receive information about what will be collected and why. You are then invited to share specific digital data relevant to the research study by accessing an AIO tool. You choose what data you are comfortable sharing before securely sending it to researchers using AIO tools.

The AIO facilitates two kinds of data donation: data download packages and screen capture.

### Data download packages

Data download packages are personal data archives that some digital platforms make available to their users. For example, digital platforms like Facebook allow users to download their posts and information associated with their account. Uber allows users to download their ride and order history, payment information, and profile information.

<details>
<summary>The six steps of a study with data download packages</summary>

1. **Brief and consent.** You will read a briefing about the project you are a part of, what data researchers are specifically looking for, and why it matters.
2. **Request.** You are provided with step-by-step instructions on how to request your data directly from the platform (Meta, Google, TikTok, X, etc.).
3. **Wait.** You then wait for the data to land in your account. This can take from just a few minutes to a few days, depending on the platform and how much information you request.
4. **Upload.** Once your data is ready, the platform emails you a link to your profile where you can download the file. Usually, the file is a regular ZIP archive. You then upload this file to our system as per the instructions. It is processed in your browser. The file has not left your computer yet, so at this point everything is done locally.
5. **Review.** Before anything is sent to the researchers, the system presents the data and allows you to review each category of what you are donating. Some fields will be pre-selected for you. If there is anything you do not want to share, you can still notify your researcher or uncheck the box before you proceed.
6. **Donate.** Once you have checked the data and confirmed your donation by clicking the submit button, it will be transferred to the research team. Everything else is discarded and never leaves your device.

</details>

Read the [data download packages explainer](/learn/resources/data-download-packages-explainer/) for more detail.

### Screen capture

Data donation using screen capture involves installing an application (phone app or browser extension) that will collect data as you use the device. It will also give you the option to review and withhold data before sharing it with researchers.

<details>
<summary>The four steps of a study with screen capture</summary>

1. **Sign up and consent.** You will read a briefing about the project you are a part of, what data researchers are specifically looking for, and why it matters.
2. **Install.** You will be provided with step-by-step instructions on how to download the application and install it.
3. **Collect and review.** While using the application you will be given the option to start and stop data collection, as well as to review the data you’ve collected. If there is anything you do not want to share, you can withdraw that data before it is uploaded.
4. **Share.** Once you have checked the data, it will be transferred to the research team. Everything else is discarded and never leaves your device.

</details>

Read the [screen capture explainer](/learn/resources/screen-capture-explainer/) for more detail, and read about the [MOT apps](/take-part/mot/).

## Keeping your data safe

We know that social media data is highly personal, and protecting it should be the AIO’s and researchers’ highest priority. Key principles include minimising the data that we collect, participant consent, research ethics, Australian-hosted infrastructure, the right to withdraw from a study and privacy protection.

Read [how we protect your data](/take-part/how-we-protect-your-data/).
