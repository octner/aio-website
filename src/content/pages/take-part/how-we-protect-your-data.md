We know that social media data is highly personal, and protecting it should be the AIO’s and researchers’ highest priority. As part of the government-funded initiative and national research infrastructure, we utilise secure, carefully designed, Australia-based solutions to manage your data.

## Our principles

- **Data minimisation.** Researchers ask for the data they need for their project and not beyond. Apps and browser extensions only collect the data needed. The system automatically filters out irrelevant data both on your device and during the upload process.
- **Participant consent.** You can review your data before sending it to the researcher, and you have the final say on which data you would like to keep private.
- **Research ethics.** We operate within ethical frameworks and ensure that our researchers have Human Research Ethics Committee approvals from their institutions before commencing their projects.
- **Australian-hosted infrastructure.** Your donated data is stored in the AIO Platform, which is a secure environment hosted on Australian servers and enabled by the Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC). It uses access controls, encryption, and other means to ensure that only approved researchers affiliated with institutions can access it.
- **The right to withdraw.** The entire data donation process is voluntary. If you change your mind after donating, we can withdraw your data from the study and securely destroy it.
- **De-identification.** Personal information like name, email address, phone numbers, and usernames are not associated with your donation.

## Who will have access to my data?

If you are a participant donating your data, only the researcher(s) who have been given explicit consent will have access to it. If you are a researcher, the data collected from your participants will be visible only to you.

## Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in an encrypted, access-controlled environment provided by the AIO via Nectar (ARDC) on Australian servers.
