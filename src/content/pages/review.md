This is the test version of the new AIO website. We ask the outreach team, researchers and the AIO leadership to review it before we build the final site.

## What we want feedback on

- **The menu.** Can you find what you came for?
- **The home page.** Does it tell you quickly what AIO is and what you can do here?
- **The wording.** Is the text clear and correct?
- **What is missing.** Does the current website have something that you need and that this test version lacks?
- **Phone use.** How does the site look and work on your phone?

## How to comment

1. **Comment on a page or the menu.** Use the buttons at the foot of the page. To comment on a part of the page, select the comment mode and click the part.
2. **Answer the eight questions.** The form below sends each answer to the shared sheet.
3. **Point to a section.** Each heading has a "#" link next to it. Copy that link into your comment to show the exact section.

## Labels on pages

A page carries a label when its text is not copied unchanged from the current site:

- **Edited from the current site:** we trimmed, merged or reordered the text of the current site.
- **New text, needs review:** the text has no source on the current site.
- **To be written:** the page or a part of it is not written yet. A box asks the page owner the open questions.

A page without a label carries the text of the current site.
