## What is the AIO Platform?

The AIO Platform is a national self-service research infrastructure. It helps researchers at Australian universities create and manage projects that investigate digital platforms and services, such as TikTok, Instagram, ChatGPT, Netflix, Facebook and Spotify.

It is designed for humanities and social sciences researchers, including those without advanced programming skills. At first it supports data donation research using Data Download Packages (DDPs): the personal data archives that people can request from digital services.

Researchers sign in with their institutional credentials. Projects with human participants or donated personal data need institutional ethics approval before data collection begins.
